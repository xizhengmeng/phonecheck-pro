Privacy Policy

Helio Daily · Last updated May 17, 2026

Helio Daily does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data stays on your device.

Data we do not collect

  • No account, email address, or personal identifiers
  • No analytics or crash reporting
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data

Data stored locally on your device

Helio stores your session history (date, duration, and start time of each logged session) locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Weather data

Helio optionally fetches current weather conditions from api.open-meteo.com, a free public weather API. This request does not include any personal identifiers, account tokens, or precise location. The API does not set cookies and does not track users. See open-meteo.com for their privacy terms.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Helio schedules local notifications on your device. These are processed entirely on-device by iOS. No notification data is sent to any server.

Third-party SDKs

Helio does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting SDKs.

Children

Helio does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is rated 4+ on the App Store.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@heliodaily.app.
