LineForge Support

LineForge turns user-owned images into printable line art, coloring pages, tattoo stencils, and craft templates.

Do I need an account?

No. The current LineForge MVP does not require an account.

Are my images uploaded?

No for MVP conversion. Images selected from Photo Library, Camera, or Files are processed locally on the device.

Where are projects stored?

Projects are stored locally in the app container. Saved project data remains on the device until you delete the app or delete a project from the Projects tab.

How do I delete a project?

Open Projects, swipe a saved project row, and choose Delete. LineForge removes the project record and the associated files it tracks for that project.

What does Pro unlock?

LineForge Pro is planned as a lifetime non-consumable unlock for watermark-free export and high-resolution PNG export. Standard watermarked export remains usable without Pro.

What should I do if Camera is unavailable?

Choose Photo Library, Files, or Use Sample Image instead. On simulators and some devices, Camera may not be available.

Contact

If you need help with LineForge, open a support request and include your device model, iOS version, and the step where you got stuck.
