Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2, 2026

Overview

PhoneCheck Pro ("the App") is a hardware inspection tool for iPhone. This policy describes how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All inspection results are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, used only during the relevant inspection step:

Camera — used to display a live preview during the camera check. No photos or videos are captured or saved.

Microphone — used to record a short audio clip for the microphone check. The recording is played back immediately and is never saved or transmitted.

Bluetooth — used only to read the Bluetooth on/off status during the connectivity check. No Bluetooth scanning or pairing occurs.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/xizhengmeng/phonecheck-pro.
