PhoneCheck Pro

Quick iPhone Hardware Check

Run 8 essential hardware tests on any iPhone in under 3 minutes. Perfect for buying or selling a used device.

What It Checks

  • Screen Colors — dead pixels, tint, burn-in
  • Touch — display response across edges and corners
  • Camera — front and rear live preview
  • Flashlight — hardware torch toggle
  • Microphone — record and instant playback
  • Speaker — audio playback test
  • Biometrics — Face ID or Touch ID support
  • Connectivity — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, battery, vibration
