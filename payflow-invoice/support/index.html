Help for PayFlow Invoice.

Find answers about creating invoices, PDF export, local reminders, privacy, and App Store review access.

Do I need an account?

No. PayFlow Invoice works without sign-in. Client, invoice, expense, and business profile records are stored locally on your device.

How do I create an invoice?

Add a client, open the Create tab, enter one or more line items, set a due date, then tap Preview Invoice. The app generates a PDF preview before you share it.

How do follow-up reminders work?

When you share and schedule an invoice, PayFlow creates local reminders around the due date and follow-up dates. These reminders stay on your device.

What happens when an invoice is paid?

Mark the invoice paid from the invoice detail or preview screen. Pending follow-up reminders for that invoice are cancelled.

Why does the app ask for notification permission?

Notification permission is used only for local invoice follow-up reminders. PayFlow does not use notifications for advertising or tracking.

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored locally on your device. This version does not include cloud sync, backend accounts, analytics, ads, or third-party payment processing.

Can App Review use the app without login?

Yes. No test account is required. Reviewers can create a sample client, create an invoice, preview the PDF, schedule reminders, mark it paid, and inspect Stats.

Need more help? Open a support request on GitHub and include your iOS version, device model, and a short description of the issue.
