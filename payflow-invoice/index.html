A focused workflow for getting paid.

PayFlow is not a full accounting suite. It is a small mobile tool for the moments freelancers repeat every week: create, share, follow up, and mark paid.

Create invoices

Add a client, line items, tax, discount, due date, notes, and payment instructions from your iPhone.

Share PDF files

Generate a clean PDF invoice and share it through the standard iOS share sheet.

Schedule follow-ups

Use local reminders for due dates and follow-up timing. No account or backend is required.

Track cashflow

See paid income, unpaid totals, overdue amounts, invoice counts, and lightweight expenses.

Private by default.

Your client and invoice records stay on your device in this version. PayFlow Invoice does not include login, analytics, advertising, cloud sync, or payment processing.

Notification permission is used only for local invoice follow-up reminders that you schedule inside the app.
