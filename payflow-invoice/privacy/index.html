Privacy Policy

PayFlow Invoice privacy policy.

Last updated: July 3, 2026. PayFlow Invoice is built as a local-first invoicing tool. This version does not collect user data.

Data collection

PayFlow Invoice does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties in this version.

Local app data

Client records, invoice details, line items, expense entries, business profile information, invoice statuses, and reminder schedules are stored locally on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, PayFlow schedules local invoice follow-up reminders on your device. Notification data is not sent to a server by PayFlow.

PDF export and sharing

PDF invoices are generated on device. If you share a PDF through iOS, your chosen sharing destination is handled by Apple and the selected app or service.

Permissions

PayFlow Invoice may request notification permission for follow-up reminders. It does not request camera, photos, contacts, precise location, or microphone access in this version.

Third-party SDKs

This version does not include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, payment processing, or crash reporting SDKs.

Children

PayFlow Invoice is a business productivity tool and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If the app later adds cloud sync, accounts, analytics, payments, or other network services, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/xizhengmeng/phonecheck-pro.
